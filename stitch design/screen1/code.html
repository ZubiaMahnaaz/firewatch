cloud_off
Phenomenon 01

Cloud & Heavy Smoke Obscuration

FN Risk: High

Thick stratus cloud decks, maritime fog, and pyrocumulonimbus plumes completely attenuate mid-infrared (3.9µm / 4.0µm) radiant flux. Sub-canopy smoldering or fast-moving fronts blanketed under dense aerosol columns may yield zero sensor signal (false negatives).

Spectral Attenuation: 3.9–4.0µm MWIR MOD14/MYD14 §2.4
schedule
Phenomenon 02

Orbit Timing & Scan Geometry

Temporal Latency

Polar-orbiting spacecraft (VIIRS on Suomi-NPP/NOAA-20/NOAA-21; MODIS on Terra/Aqua) achieve sun-synchronous nadir overpasses roughly 2 to 4 times per 24h diurnal cycle. Rapid diurnal ignitions occurring between orbital tracks remain undetected until the subsequent transit.

Window Interval: 3–6 hrs between passes Suomi-NPP / NOAA-20
crop_free
Phenomenon 03

Pixel Footprint & Geolocation

Nadir: 375m / 1000m

VIIRS I-band offers 375m nadir resolution; MODIS is 1km. Off-nadir scans experience optical bow-tie distortion and pixel elongation. Coordinate pins signify the geometric pixel centroid, never the sub-pixel flaming front perimeter or exact boundary line.

Spatial Error: ±187.5m (VIIRS) to ±500m Bow-tie Correction
factory
Phenomenon 04

Industrial Confounders & Refraction

FP Risk: High

Natural gas flaring, petrochemical catalytic crackers, scrap-metal foundries, and solar glare glinting from corrugated metal agriculture roofing frequently mimic thermal radiometric brightness signatures, triggering elevated Fire Radiative Power (FRP).

Known Artifacts: Static Flare Inventory MODIS Thermal Mask

False-Positive Mitigation Pipeline

STAGE 1-3

Raw FIRMS thermal radiative vectors pass through a deterministic three-tiered heuristic filter to deprioritize industrial noise and non-wildfire radiative energy.

1
Static Heat Signature Masking 1.2km Excl.

Automated spatial join against the Global Gas Flare Catalog (GGFR) and NOAA persistent industrial thermal source database. Hits within 1,200m buffer are tagged CONF_FP_INDUSTRIAL and downweighted.

2
Land Cover Zoning Cross-Ref USGS NLCD

Cross-validation against USGS National Land Cover Database 30m rasters. Identifies agricultural crop clearing, controlled burn permits, and open-pit aggregate mines versus wildland-urban interface (WUI) canopy fuels.

3
Human Analyst Triage (Tier-1) Duty Desk

Manual geospatial review of GOES-16/18 ABI full-disk 5-minute cadence imagery, paired with near-ground automated weather stations (RAWS) wind vectors before priority escalation to emergency operations dispatch.
